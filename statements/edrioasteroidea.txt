"Vanuxem" refers to Lardner Vanuxem

Lardner Vanuxem was born in 1792 and died in 1843

Lardner Vanuxem authored the 1842 book "Geology of New-York. Part III. Comprising the Survey of the Third Geological District", in which he made the following statements:

on page 158, New genus Agelacrinites belongs to Crinoidea
on page 158, New species Hamiltonensis belongs to Agelacrinites
on page 306, figure 80 illustrates Agelacrinites Hamiltonensis


"Forbes" refers to Edward Forbes

Edward Forbes was born in 1815 and died in 1854

Edward Forbes authored the 1848 article "On the Cystideæ of the Silurian Rocks of the British Islands" in Volume II, Part 2 of the Memoirs of the Geological Survey of Great Britain, in which he made the following statements:

On page 483, the Cystideæ are an order of radiated animals
On page 486, the Cystideæ are separate from the Crinoideæ
On page 494, section B of the Cystideæ are defined to have bodies composed of an indefinite number of plates

On page 494, Agelocrinites belongs to section B of the Cystideæ
On page 519, Agelacrinites belongs to Cystideæ
On page 520, Agelacrinites is defined as by Lardner Vanuxem on page 168 of the 1842 book "Geology of New-York. Part III. Comprising the Survey of the Third Geological District"
In the previous statement, the page number of 168 is an error; the correct page number is 158.
On page 521, New species Buchianus belongs to Agelacrinites

On page 527, Echinidæ is an Order of Echinodermata
On page 527, Ophiuridæ is an Order of Echinodermata
On page 527, Asteriadæ is an Order of Echinodermata
On page 527, Cystideæ is an Order of Echinodermata
On page 527, Crinoideæ is an Order of Echinodermata
