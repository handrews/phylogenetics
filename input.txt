Breimer and Ubaghs' use of discarded or unused higher taxa names to promote strict priority over currently accepted usage and their establishment of a new category level (superclass) for stalked echinoderms only add to the problem of classifying echinoderms and do little to maintain stability."""

Also notes that "Echmatocrinus" was recently shown to be an octocoral, not the oldest supported crinoid.  Most likely sister group to crinoids are fistuliporite rhombiferans, based on stem, cup, and arm morphological similarities.

Crinoid arms are homologous to asteroid arms, but arms did not simply evolve once. 1998-06-20-a Bilateral homologues in echinoderms and a predictive model of the bilateral echinoderm ancestor.pdf
Morris, V.B.


Definition of the bilateral echinodem ancestor

The bilateral echinoderm ancestor (Fig. 1G) was elongate with an anterior mouth and posterior anus on the ventral side of the body. A paired nerve cord also on the ventral side of the body extended from mouth to anus but diverged anteriorly to form a nerve ring encircling the mouth. Long coelomic sacs lay on either side of the nerve ring and paired nerve cord. Protrusions from these sacs formed a metameric series of paired tube feet along the length of the body ventrally, innervated from the nerve ring and paired nerve cord. Anteriorly, each coelomic sac ended in a madreporite-like connexion to the exterior.
