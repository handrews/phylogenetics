## Systematic acts printed

Printed classification (p. 418):
```
Class EDRIOASTEROIDEA Billings, 1858
Order STROMATOCYSTITIDA Bell, 1980
Family STROMATOCYSTITIDAE Bassler, 1935
Genus Stromatocystites Pompeckj, 1896
```

The paper states (p. 418): "Six species of *Stromatocystites* have been proposed to date," then works through each:

1. ***S. pentangularis* Pompeckj, 1896** — the type species, from the middle Cambrian of Bohemia; **retained** as valid, and gains a new synonym (below). Also identified by Smith (1985) in the lower Cambrian of Newfoundland.
2. **"*Trochocystites cannati*" Miquel, 1894** — originally described in a different genus, later moved to *Stromatocystites* by Miquel (1905) and "with doubt" by Thoral (1935), "before becoming the type species of the genus *Cambraster* (Cabibel et al., 1959)." This is recounted history, not an act of the present paper: by the time of this paper the species is already in *Cambraster*, not *Stromatocystites*, and the paper does not revisit it.
3. ***S. balticus* Jaekel, 1899** — p. 420: "Smith (1985) suggested *S. balticus* as a nomen dubium. We agree with Smith (1985) that the original description from Jaekel (1899) is too vague." This paper does **not itself declare** the nomen dubium (that act belongs to Smith 1985); it explicitly endorses/reaffirms it. It then adds a new tentative identification (p. 420): "we tentatively suggest that these two specimens are probably similar to those described herein from Sweden, suggesting *S. balticus* should also be treated as *Stromatocystites* cf. *pentangularis*." So the name stays a nomen dubium, but the underlying (lost) material is tentatively equated with *S. pentangularis* material under open nomenclature.
4. ***S. walcotti* Schuchert, 1919** — p. 418: "it appears as a valid species of *Stromatocystites*." **Retained** as valid.
5. ***S. reduncus* Smith and Jell, 1990** — discussed comparatively (p. 418) and included, unflagged, in the summary list of valid species below. **Retained** as valid.
6. ***S. flexibilis* Parsley and Prokop, 2004** — p. 418: "Finally, Parsley and Prokop (2004) described *Stromatocystites flexibilis* in the middle Cambrian of Bohemia. However, all morphological differences between *S. flexibilis* and *S. pentangularis* (e.g., loosely articulated parts of the theca, slightly elevated ambulacral area and size of thecal plates) may only represent the result of different taphonomic preservation. **This species is thus reinterpreted here as a junior synonym of *S. pentangularis***." A new synonymy act by this paper, also stated in the abstract (p. 417): "*S. flexibilis* is reinterpreted as a junior synonym of *S. pentangularis*."

Summary statement (p. 418), the paper's own tally: "In summary, only three species of Cambrian edrioasteroids can be assigned to the genus *Stromatocystites*: *S. pentangularis*, *S. walcotti*, and *S. reduncus*. Other occurrences of possible *Stromatocystites* are reported below."

New open-nomenclature material described (not new species — no "n. sp." anywhere in the paper):

- **"*Stromatocystites* cf. *pentangularis*"** (p. 418) — new material from Turkey (Koruk Fm.) and Sweden (Kvarntorp), described and figured, identified with a qualified "cf." to the type species.
- **"*Stromatocystites* sp. A"** (p. 421) — three specimens from Poland, previously misassigned: originally figured by Dzik and Orłowski (1995) as part of the ctenocystoid "*Jugoszovia archaeocyathoides*" (a chimaera later exposed by Domínguez Alonso 1999a) and subsequently reinterpreted as "Stromatocystitidae indet." by Domínguez Alonso (1999b). This paper: "The three specimens from the Holy Cross Mountains are here assigned to the genus *Stromatocystites*… However, the Polish material is left here in open nomenclature (*Stromatocystites* sp. A) because of incomplete preservation, which prevents any complete description." A synonymy-style citation list is printed for this material (p. 421): "v. 1995. *Jugoszovia archaeocyathoides* -Dzik and Orłowski, fig. 3, Pl. 1, fig. 8, Pl. 2, figs. 1–3." and "v. 1999b. Stromatocystitidae indet. -Domínguez Alonso, Pl. 3, figs. 8, 9" (the "v." prefix is the standard "vide"/figured-specimen convention).
- **"*Stromatocystites* sp."** (p. 421–422) — a single specimen from Riosol, Spain, described and left "in open nomenclature" for lack of preservation detail.

No new combination ("comb. nov.") is printed anywhere by this paper — all species transfers discussed (e.g., "*Trochocystites cannati*" → *Stromatocystites* → *Cambraster*) are historical and attributed to earlier authors.

## Stated classification of *Stromatocystites*

As printed (p. 418, quoted above): Class Edrioasteroidea Billings, 1858 > Order Stromatocystitida Bell, 1980 > Family Stromatocystitidae Bassler, 1935 > Genus *Stromatocystites* Pompeckj, 1896. Diagnostic remarks (p. 418) distinguish the genus from *Cambraster*, *Kailidiscus*, *Walcottidiscus*, basal isorophids (*Protorophus*, *Chatsworthia*, *Hadrodiscus*), *Totiglobus*, and *Aragocystites*, all by ambulacral-plating and aboral-surface characters — these are comparative remarks, not classification acts.
