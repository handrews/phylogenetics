In his proposed classification and cladogram, Derstler (1975, 1979) separates the Phyllocystidae from the other Cornuta (see also Cripps 1989). His definition of this family applies to Lobocarpus, except that its theca is less symmetrical than that of Phyllocystis. However, as it is to this family that Lobocarpus is closest, it is suggested that it be placed there, but with the reservations imposed by an incomplete knowledge of its morphology.
